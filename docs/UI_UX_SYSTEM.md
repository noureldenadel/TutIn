# UI/UX System — TutIn Study Companion

> Living source of truth for TutIn's new UI direction, visual identity, interaction design, and reusable component patterns.

**Development Scope & Maintenance Rule:**
- **Active Canvas:** TutIn v7 lives directly in the repository root (`client/` and `server/`, with the legacy v5 UI retired).
- **Rule of Record:** Review this document before proposing or implementing UI changes in `client/`. Update this document whenever a decision, component convention, or token is refined or established.

---

## 1. Product Experience

- **Product Purpose:** A calm, local-first learning space and video study companion for masterclasses, technical lectures, and self-paced courses.
- **Primary Users:** Self-directed learners, students, and professionals studying from local video collections and imported online content.
- **Core User Journeys:**
  1. **Course Library & Discovery:** Browsing courses, checking progress, searching/filtering, and importing new folders or links.
  2. **Active Study & Playback:** Focused video playback with synchronized transcripts, smart notes, flashcards, and quizzes.
  3. **Visual Branding & Customization:** Generating distinct mathematical vector artwork for courses without uploaded covers.
  4. **Curriculum & Analytics:** Managing video playlists, tracking learning consistency, and reviewing instructor profiles.
- **Desired Experience:** Distraction-free workstation aesthetic (inspired by Linear, Raycast, and macOS). Clean, technical, calm, and uncluttered. High cognitive economy with zero visual noise.

---

## 2. Core Design Principles

1. **Two-Pane Workstation Split:** The workspace uses a pinned vertical dock (`IconDock`, `sticky top-0 h-dvh w-16`, `bg-shell`) separated by a subtle hairline divider (`border-l border-border/50`) from an edge-to-edge `<main>` workspace surface (`bg-background`).
2. **Copy Economy ("Say less, show more"):** If the UI already communicates meaning, extra text is clutter. Remove redundant helper text, section intros, and repeated verbs (e.g. use "Course folder" and "YouTube", never "Add from YouTube").
3. **Solid Recognition Over Wireframe Reading:** Use solid, high-contrast silhouettes and established iconography to speed up recognition.
   - **No emojis** anywhere in the UI.
   - **No icons in decorative colored circles** or rounded squares.
   - Primary recognition targets (e.g. source types) use **flat solid fills** (`fill-current text-foreground`) rather than faint 1px outlines.
   - Brand icons use **authentic geometric marks** in **flat monochrome**—never multi-colored brand logos (no red YouTube, no tricolor Google Drive).
4. **Concentric Curvature:** Radiuses scale down logically from outer containers to inner elements. Cards and dialogs are 12px (`rounded-xl`), inputs/trays are 8px (`rounded-lg`), buttons/items are 6px (`rounded-md`), and micro-chips/inner segmented tabs are 4px (`rounded-sm`).
5. **Deterministic Aesthetics:** Courses without custom artwork receive generative vector thumbnails seeded deterministically from their course ID and metadata—zero network requests, always sharp at any screen resolution.

### Iconography Rules
- **Solid Silhouettes:** For media sources, course import types, and key quick-recognition targets, use solid vector silhouettes (`size-4 shrink-0 fill-current text-foreground`) instead of faint stroked lines.
- **Authentic Brand Marks:** Always use the real, official mark geometry (e.g. exact Google Drive cyclic band vector, authentic YouTube badge with triangle cutout). Never improvise or distort brand logos.
- **Monochrome Flat Tone:** All icons inherit the current foreground or muted-foreground color. Do not use brand colors (red, green, blue, yellow) which create visual fragmentation and cheapen the UI.
- **No Decorative Surrounds:** Never wrap an icon in a colored circle or pill container simply to make it visually busy.
- **No Emojis:** Emojis are strictly banned from UI labels, buttons, headers, and tabs.

### Typography
- **Primary Typeface:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` with fallback to `system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`.
- **Monospace Typeface:** `ui-monospace, Consolas, monospace` for timestamps, duration badges, and seeds.
- **Hierarchy:**
  - Page Titles: `text-2xl font-bold sm:text-3xl`
  - Card & Section Titles: `text-lg font-semibold leading-7`
  - Subheadings / Group Labels: `text-sm font-semibold`
  - Body Text: `text-sm font-normal text-muted-foreground`
  - Microcopy / Badges: `text-xs` or `text-[11px] font-mono font-semibold`
- **Single-Line Truncation Rule:** Course titles in cards must terminate on a single line (`truncate`) with an ellipsis and include `title={course.title}` to display a native browser tooltip on hover. Never use multiline clamp in card grids to ensure consistent card heights.

### Shapes & Corner Radiuses (The 4-Tier Hierarchy)
Defined in `src/styles.css` under `@theme inline` with strict geometric roles to prevent the "all rounded squares" look:

1. **Structural Cards & Dialogs (12px / Subtle Rounded Rectangles):**
   - `--radius-xl` (**12px**): Course cards, modal dialogs (`DialogContent`), settings modal, video screen bezel.
   - *Rule:* Anchors cards and modal surfaces; never wrap the top-level `<main>` workspace in a rounded outer frame or use pill radiuses on cards.
2. **Interactive Tooling & Controls (6px – 8px / Technical Rectangles):**
   - `--radius-lg` (**8px**): Search input, dock action buttons, dropdown panels, segmented control outer tracks.
   - `--radius-md` (**6px**): Standard action buttons (`+ Add Course`), menu items, tooltip bubbles.
   - *Rule:* Retains crisp tool-like feel (Linear/Raycast aesthetic). Do not use pill buttons by default.
3. **Concentric Nested Curvature ($R_{\text{inner}} = R_{\text{outer}} - \text{padding}$):**
   - `--radius-sm` (**4px**): Inner segmented tab buttons (e.g. `All | In Progress | Completed`), video duration tags (`04:20:00`), progress tracks.
   - *Rule:* When an 8px (`rounded-lg`) track has 4px padding (`p-1`), inner tab buttons must use 4px (`rounded-sm`) so the inner corners do not bulge.
4. **Human Identity & Floating Accents (Full Circle / `rounded-full`):**
   - Instructor avatars (both photos and uppercase initials), central video play button, volume slider thumb.
   - *Rule:* Avatars represent people; always style them as circles (`rounded-full`) to immediately break the rectangular grid.
5. **Passive Metadata & Status Badges (Pills / `rounded-full`):**
   - Topic tags (`"UI Design"`), version indicators (`"Version 1.0.0"`), live engine badges, and `"Playing"` status capsules.
   - *Rule:* Capsules signal read-only status and tags, clearly differentiating them from clickable square buttons.

### Elevation, Tonal Layering & Shadows
- **Tonal Separation Over Wireframe Box Strokes (With Architectural Dividers):** Outer containers, cards, inputs, and dialogs separate primarily through calibrated surface stepping (`--shell` → `--background` → `--card` + inset `--secondary`) and soft resting elevation rather than 1px wireframe boxes (`border border-border`). Interactive focus and selected states use soft `ring-1` / `ring-2` (`focus-within:ring-2`, `ring-primary/35`). However, **subtle divider lines** (`border-border/50`, `divide-border/50`) are explicitly used wherever structural separation is needed—such as between toolbars and editors, headers and scrollable lists, footers and content, module accordions, navigation columns and content panes, and subheader controls above grids.
- `--shadow-card`: Subtle resting depth with crisp hairline ring separation for borderless cards and floating panels.
  - Light: `0 1px 3px 0 oklch(0.2 0.012 258 / 0.07), 0 0 0 1px oklch(0.2 0.012 258 / 0.045)`
  - Dark: `0 2px 8px 0 oklch(0 0 0 / 0.32), 0 0 0 1px oklch(1 0 0 / 0.055)`
  - *Rule:* Avoid deep, dark fuzzy halos (no wide 28–32px blurs). Cards stay crisp and grounded.
- `--shadow-workspace`: `0 18px 48px oklch(0.2 0.012 258 / 0.11), 0 0 0 1px oklch(0.2 0.012 258 / 0.06)` (light) / `0 22px 60px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.45), 0 0 0 1px oklch(1 0 0 / 0.08)` (dark) — Reserved for dialogs, popovers, dropdown menus, and floating canvas panels. Never apply to individual cards on hover.

### Scrollbar Architecture & Non-Overlapping Gutters
- **Universal Custom Styling:** Replaced default blocky OS scrollbars with sleek, floating pill thumbs (`width: 8px`, `border: 2px solid transparent`, `background-clip: padding-box`). The thumb floats centered inside an invisible track with 2px inset breathing space.
- **Non-Overlapping Gutters (`scrollbar-gutter: stable`):** Dedicated gutter space is permanently reserved along the scroll edge of scrollable `<main>` views and internal scroll panels. Content cards, search inputs, buttons, and text never slide underneath the thumb, and switching between long and short views produces zero horizontal layout shifting.
- **Utility System:**
  - `custom-scrollbar`: Standard 8px width with `scrollbar-gutter: stable` for scrollable `<main>` pages and the settings modal.
  - `custom-scrollbar-sm`: Compact 6px width without `scrollbar-gutter: stable` so internal sidebar panels (`px-3`) maintain symmetric left/right horizontal alignment with their headers.
  - `scrollbar-none`: Eliminates visual scrollbars on horizontal navigation bars (e.g. mobile settings tabs).
- **Single Scroll Responsibility:** Avoid nested `overflow-y-auto` containers inside `<main>` on standard views; `AppShell` mounts persistently in `routes/__root.tsx` and automatically applies `overflow-hidden` (`h-full`) on full-bleed viewport tools (`/canvas/$courseId`, `/roadmap`) so unneeded scrollbar gutters are eliminated.

## 4. Component System

### 1. App Shell (`src/components/layout/app-shell.tsx`)
- **Structure:** Persistent root layout (`routes/__root.tsx`) rendering an edge-to-edge flex frame bounded by viewport height (`flex h-dvh flex-col overflow-hidden bg-shell text-foreground md:flex-row`), so navigating across routes never unmounts `IconDock`, theme state, or shared dialogs.
- **Navigation Dock (`IconDock`):**
  - **Desktop (`>= 768px`, `md:`):** Pinned permanently to the left (`md:order-1 md:sticky md:top-0 md:h-dvh md:w-16 md:flex-col`). Contains the animated brand logo (`DotLogo` in `src/components/layout/dot-logo.tsx`, while browser tab/app metadata uses `public/tutin-icon.svg`), route navigation icons with a persistently mounted, GPU-composited (`translate3d` over `250ms cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`, respecting `prefers-reduced-motion`) sliding `bg-accent` capsule and side indicator bar (`h-5 w-1 rounded-full bg-foreground/70`), subtle tactile icon press (`active:scale-95`), theme toggle, and settings trigger.
  - **Mobile (`< 768px`, `max-md`):** Docked at the bottom of the screen (`order-2 flex h-14 w-full shrink-0 flex-row items-center justify-around border-t border-border/50 bg-shell px-2 pb-[env(safe-area-inset-bottom)]`) with `<nav>` and the utility wrapper set to `contents md:flex` so `Home`, `Instructors`, `History`, `Statistics`, `Roadmap`, and `Settings` (plus active AI jobs `DotLogo` popover when `activeJobs.length > 0`; idle top `DotLogo` link is `hidden md:flex`) distribute evenly across the bottom bar. The dark mode toggle button is hidden on mobile (`hidden md:flex`, configurable via `Settings -> General -> Appearance`), active items render `max-md:bg-accent` with a bottom indicator pill (`md:hidden`), and hover tooltips are suppressed on mobile (`hidden md:block`).
- **Workspace Surface (`<main>`):** Full-bleed edge-to-edge workspace (`order-1 min-h-0 min-w-0 flex-1 bg-background md:order-2 md:h-dvh md:border-l md:border-border/50`) above the mobile bottom bar and beside the desktop left dock with no outer card margins, rounded corners, or shell shadow, paired with directional route entry animations (`animate-page-enter-down` / `animate-page-enter-up` for scrollable pages, opacity-only `animate-page-fade` for fixed-viewport canvases and same-nav subroutes) keyed by `pathname` while restoring per-route scroll from `sessionStorage`. Standard routes use `overflow-y-auto overflow-x-hidden custom-scrollbar`; full-bleed interactive whiteboards (`/canvas/$courseId`, `/roadmap`) derive fixed viewport (`overflow-hidden`).

### 2. Course Card (`CourseCard`)
- **Composition:** 16:9 ratio thumbnail + text body + progress bar.
- **Card Hover Animation & Menu Continuity:**
  - Smooth card lift on hover and while the card menu is active: `transition-transform duration-300 hover:-translate-y-1 data-[menu-open=true]:-translate-y-1` (retaining `data-menu-open="true"` across Radix dropdown exit animation so opening and `Escape`-closing never causes hover drop/re-hover flicker).
  - **Borderless Surface:** Cards sit on elevated `bg-card` with subtle resting `shadow-card` and no wireframe outer border.
  - **No Hover Shadow:** Individual cards maintain their flat, subtle resting depth (`shadow-card`) and never produce hard hover drop shadows (`hover:shadow-xl` banned).
  - Title hover transition: `group-hover:text-foreground/80 group-data-[menu-open=true]:text-foreground/80 dark:group-hover:text-white dark:group-data-[menu-open=true]:text-white transition-colors duration-200`.
- **Thumbnail Layer & Hover Zoom:**
  - Thumbnail container with `aspect-video overflow-hidden`.
  - Artwork zooms subtly on hover: `size-full transition-transform duration-700 ease-out group-hover:scale-105 group-data-[menu-open=true]:scale-105`.
  - Clean artwork at rest with no bottom dark gradient scrim (`CourseThumbnail` and `CourseCard` both render cover images and generated SVGs flat without `from-black/*` overlays); hovering activates a full-surface soft dimming veil (`bg-black/30 opacity-0 transition-opacity duration-300 group-hover:opacity-100 group-data-[menu-open=true]:opacity-100`) behind the central action button.
- **Central Action Button (Glassmorphic, Borderless):**
  - Smooth frosted floating action circle: `size-14 rounded-full flex items-center justify-center bg-card/85 dark:bg-white/15 opacity-0 scale-90 backdrop-blur-md transition-all duration-200 ease-out group-hover:scale-100 group-hover:opacity-100 group-data-[menu-open=true]:scale-100 group-data-[menu-open=true]:opacity-100`. Zero harsh borders or drop shadows.
  - Video Courses: `<Play className="size-6 text-foreground dark:text-white" fill="currentColor" />` (no extra horizontal margin/translation needed as Lucide's `Play` path is already optically weighted inside its `24x24` viewBox).
  - Direct Link Courses: `<Link2 className="size-6 text-foreground dark:text-white" />`.
- **Dual-Route Card Interaction:**
  - Direct Link Courses (`sourceType: "link"` or `courseUrl`): Card hit area is an anchor tag (`<a>`) opening the destination in a new tab (`target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`), with no persistent source badge overlaid on the thumbnail.
  - Video Courses: Card hit area is `<Link to="/learn/$courseId">` navigating to the internal player.
- **Metadata, Duration Tag & Completion Semantics:** Single-line truncated title (`truncate`), optional instructor chip with uppercase initials (hidden altogether—including no `"Instructor"` or `"Self-paced"` fallback—when `course.instructor.trim()` is empty; `flex h-full flex-col` + `mt-auto pt-5` keep grid row progress bars aligned), progress bar, lesson counter, and `HH:MM:SS` duration badge (`formatDurationClock`, e.g., `02:33:00`, centered vertically via `inline-flex h-5 items-center justify-center` + `.duration-badge-text`). Progress bars and percentage labels stay calm neutral while in progress (`bg-foreground/55`, `text-foreground/75`) and shift to desaturated emerald (`bg-chart-4`, `text-chart-4`) when a course reaches `100%` completion.
- **Action Menu:** Compact frosted-glass 3-dot trigger (`MoreVertical`, `size-8 rounded-lg`, `bg-card/85 dark:bg-white/15 backdrop-blur-md`) anchored in the top-right corner of the thumbnail (`top-3 right-3 z-20`), aligned with the `bottom-3 right-3` duration tag. Hidden at rest on hover-capable devices (`opacity-0`) and revealed cleanly on card hover, `focus-visible`, or open menu without dark opaque patches over artwork. Uses `<DropdownMenu modal={false}>` paired with `data-menu-open` grace continuity so opening the menu and closing via `Escape` keeps card hover active without un-hover/re-hover flicker; leaves card titles full width below. Automatically adapts actions based on source type (`Open link` for external URLs; `Curriculum`, `Rescan folder`, and `Show in Explorer` for local video courses).

### 3. Generative Artwork & Cover Source Engine (`src/lib/thumbnail-generator.ts` & `course-thumbnail.tsx`)
- **Purpose:** Manages thumbnail presentation across the application with dual capabilities: rendering real uploaded/auto-detected course cover images (`courses.thumbnail_data` or `.tutin/` cover files) or automatically generating distinct, algorithmic SVG vector artwork when no custom cover is set.
- **Algorithms:** Supports 4 deterministic geometric patterns (`0` Binary ramp column grid, `1` Thin strips horizontal cuts, `2` Block rows asymmetric blocks, `3` Ramp rows logarithmic stepped rows) selected from `seed % 4`.
- **Fixed Preset Parameters:** Uses the preserved `DEFAULT_GENERATOR_SETTINGS` configuration (`hue: 150`, `drift: 25`, `density: 36`, `variance: 50`, `contrast: 60`, `spark: 20`, `bands: 3`, `pattern: "auto"`, `lockHue: false`) without runtime customization overrides.

### 4. Add Course Control (`AddCourseControl`)
- **Split Button Pattern:** Primary `+ Add Course` button (defaults to local folder import) paired with a dropdown chevron.
- **Dropdown Menu:** Clean, decluttered single list without repeated "Add from" verbs and without dividing separator lines or categorical headers (`LOCAL`/`WEB` removed):
  - `Course folder` (Flat solid folder icon: `viewBox="0 0 24 24"`)
  - `YouTube` (Flat solid YouTube badge with play cutout: `viewBox="0 0 24 24"`)
  - `Google Drive` (Authentic flat monochrome cyclic 3-segment band mark: `viewBox="0 0 192 192"` with opacity steps)
  - `Direct link` (Flat solid globe mark: `viewBox="0 0 24 24"`)

### 5. Instructor Avatar System (`src/components/layout/instructor-avatar.tsx` & `thumbnail-generator.ts`)
- **Centralized Deterministic Math:** Uses `getAvatarColors(palette)` and `getInstructorPalette(name, course)` (`bg` + `fg` + `spark` tokens) derived deterministically from instructor/course seed math over the 5-tone palette (`L = [13, 29, 46, 62, 88]`).
- **Fixed Tone Pair:** Uses preserved Tone 4 background (`avatarBgTone: 3`) and Tone 2 initials (`avatarFgTone: 1`).
- **Brand Typography (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Initials render in SVG `<text>` using `var(--font-sans), 'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, sans-serif` (`500` weight, `8` glyph size) for open glyph counters inside the circular boundary.
- **Cross-Screen Consistency:** Uploaded photos persist to SQLite (`/api/instructors`) and hydrate across the library, player, and instructor views via `tutin:instructor_avatar_changed`.

## 5. Page & Layout Patterns

### Home / Dashboard (`/`)

## 6. UX Writing & Copy Economy

- **Buttons:** 1 to 2 words, verb-first ("Add Course", "Resume", "Save", "Reset defaults", "Import videos", "Instructors").
- **Nav Items & Tabs:** 1 word where possible ("Home", "Instructors", "History", "Statistics", "Roadmap", "Playlist", "Notes", "Transcript", "General", "AI", "Voice", "Data", "About").
- **Dropdown Items:** Clean nouns ("Course folder", "YouTube", "Google Drive", "Direct link", "Curriculum", "Rescan folder").
- **Empty States:** Exactly 1 short sentence plus 1 action button. No paragraphs.
- **Dialogs:** The dialog title is the direct action or question; consequence is capped at one short sentence.
- **Section Intros:** Do not add introductory sentences or paragraphs under headings if the controls explain themselves.
- **Metrics & Stats:** Avoid repeating the label in the metric value (e.g. "Completed lessons: 128 / 180", not "128 / 180 Lessons").
