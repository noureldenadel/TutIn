## [7.0.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added, Changed & Removed
- **Optical Vertical Centering for Video Captions (`client/src/components/player/video-caption-overlay.tsx`, `docs/UI_UX_SYSTEM.md`):**
  - Replaced symmetric `py-[0.06em]` / `leading-[1.46]` on `VideoCaptionOverlay` (`inline box-decoration-clone`) with font-metric-balanced `pt-[0.02em] pb-[0.14em]` and `leading-[1.56]`, compensating `Segoe UI` / sans-serif ascender-vs-descender headroom (`~0.38em` above cap-height vs `~0.25em` below baseline) so caption text sits optically centered inside each dark background plate without clipping descenders (`g`, `j`, `p`, `q`, `y`) or breaking per-line wrapping.
